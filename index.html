What’s Jev?

Most AI models write. Jev, from TypeSafe AI, decides. Give it some text and a question with a fixed set of answers, and it picks one and tells you how sure it is. It’s a System 1 model: a quick gut call, not a long chain of reasoning.

System 1

“What color is the sky?”

Blue. Instant, no thinking needed.

System 2

“Why is the sky blue?”

Now you have to reason it through. That’s what the big chat models are for.

Jev does the first kind: fast answers to questions with a fixed set of answers.

  • FastAbout a fifth of a second per call.
  • CheapAbout $0.04 per million input tokens, so you can check everything, not a sample.
  • Three kinds of questionYes/no, a rating on your scale, or one pick from your list.
  • Not for writingNo summaries or explanations. Your code decides what to do with the answer.

Why speed matters: a voice agent

Someone calls an airline’s AI phone agent. Before the agent can say anything useful, it has to know what the caller wants: a new flight, a refund, a lost bag, or a human. Every moment spent working that out is silence on the line.
